## Inserting Three Playlist Songs

Insert three songs into the `Playlist` table. Durations are stored in seconds:

```sql
INSERT INTO Playlist (id, song_name, artist, duration)
VALUES
	(2, 'As It Was', 'Harry Styles', 167),
	(3, 'Levitating', 'Dua Lipa', 203),
	(4, 'Heat Waves', 'Glass Animals', 238);
```
