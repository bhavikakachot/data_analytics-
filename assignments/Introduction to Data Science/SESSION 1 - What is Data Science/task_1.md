# 1. YouTube 
 YouTube analyzes your 
 - watch history, 
 - searches, 
 - likes,
 - subscriptions,
 - how long you watch a video.
 
  It uses these signals to recommend videos that are likely to match your interests, making it easier to discover useful or entertaining content.

# 2. Google Maps
 Google Maps analyzes your:
- Current location
- Live traffic conditions
- Road speed and traffic patterns
- Distance to the destination
- Previous travel-time data

It uses this data to suggest the fastest route, estimate your arrival time, and warn you about traffic jams or road delays.

# 3. Amazon
Amazon analyzes your:
- Search history
- Products you view
- Previous purchases
- Items added to your cart or wishlist
- Product ratings and reviews

It uses these signals to recommend products you may be interested in. For example, if you search for a laptop, Amazon may suggest a laptop bag, mouse, headphones, or similar laptops.
