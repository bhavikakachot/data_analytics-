
## Data Science in E-commerce

**Industry: E-commerce**

One innovative way data science is being used in e-commerce today is **personalized product recommendations**.

E-commerce companies collect customer data such as-
- **search history,**
- **products viewed,**
- **clicks,**
- **add-to-cart activity,**
- **previous purchases**

 Machine learning algorithms analyze this data to understand customer preferences and predict which products a customer is most likely to be interested in. The website can then show personalized products such as **“Recommended for you”** or **“You may also like.”** 

A real example is **Paytm Mall**. Paytm uses customer data and machine learning to generate personalized product recommendations for more than **10 million daily visitors**. Its personalized homepage achieved a **6% conversion rate, three times higher than its previous recommendation system**.

Another example is **Meesho**, an Indian e-commerce platform. Meesho developed a machine-learning-based feed-ranking system that considers users' **preferences, browsing patterns, previous shopping behavior, geography, and other factors** to personalize the products shown in each user's feed.

**In conclusion,** data science helps e-commerce companies understand customer behavior and provide personalized shopping experiences. This can help customers find relevant products faster while also increasing **engagement, conversions, and sales**.
