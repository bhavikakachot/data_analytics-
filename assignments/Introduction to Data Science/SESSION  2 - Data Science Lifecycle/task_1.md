# Data Science Project Problem Definition

# **App: Flipkart**

## **Problem Definition:**
Flipkart users sometimes face late deliveries, especially when there is high order volume, long delivery distances, seller delays, or courier-related issues. This can lead to customer dissatisfaction, complaints, order cancellations, and poor customer experience.

## **Business Goal:**
The goal is to predict whether an order will be delivered late before the expected delivery date. This can help Flipkart identify high-risk orders early, improve delivery planning, provide more accurate delivery estimates, reduce delays and complaints, and improve customer satisfaction.

## **Data Needed:**
- Order date and time
- Expected delivery date
- Actual delivery date
- Seller location
- Customer location
- Distance between seller and customer
- Product category
- Seller's previous delivery performance
- Delivery partner/courier details
- Number of active orders
- Historical delivery times
- Order cancellation data
- Weather and traffic conditions

## **Expected Outcome:**
A data science model can predict whether an order is likely to be delivered late or on time. Flipkart can use this prediction to identify delayed orders early, improve delivery planning, provide more accurate delivery estimates, and take corrective action to reduce late deliveries.
