## LAN, MAN, WAN

## 1. LAN (Local Area Network)

**Definition:**

> **LAN is a network that connects computers and devices within a small geographical area like a home, school, or office.**

**Features:**
- Covers a small area.
- High speed data transfer.
- Easy to maintain.

**Examples:**
- Home Wi-Fi network
- Office network
- School computer lab

---

## 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

**Definition:**

> **MAN is a network that connects computers and devices across a city or large campus area.**

**Features:**
- Covers a larger area than LAN.
- Connects multiple LANs.
- Medium speed compared to LAN and WAN.

**Examples:**
- City-wide internet network
- University campus network

---

## 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

**Definition:**

> **WAN is a network that connects computers and devices over a large geographical area such as countries or continents.**

**Features:**
- Covers the largest area.
- Uses telephone lines, satellites, and fiber connections.
- More complex and expensive.

**Examples:**
- Internet
- Bank networks across countries

---

## Difference Between LAN, MAN, and WAN

| Network | Full Form | Coverage Area | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LAN` | Local Area Network | Small area | Home, Office |
| `MAN` | Metropolitan Area Network | City area | City network |
| `WAN` | Wide Area Network | Country/World | Internet |

## Simple Line:

> **LAN = Small area, MAN = City area, WAN = Large area (World).**
