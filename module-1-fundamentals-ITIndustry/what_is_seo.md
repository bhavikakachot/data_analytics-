## SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

**Definition:**

> **SEO is the process of improving a website to increase its visibility and ranking on search engines like Google, Bing, and Yahoo.**

## Full Form:

- **SEO = Search Engine Optimization**

## Purpose of SEO:

- Helps websites appear higher in search results.
- Increases website traffic.
- Improves user experience.
- Helps businesses reach more customers.

## Types of SEO:

- **1. On-Page SEO**
  - Optimizes content and elements inside a website.
  - Examples: `Keywords`, `Titles`, `Headings`, `Content`

- **2. Off-Page SEO**
  - Improves website ranking using activities outside the website.
  - Examples: `Backlinks`, `Social Media Sharing`

- **3. Technical SEO**
  - Improves website technical performance.
  - Examples: `Website Speed`, `Mobile Friendly`, `Security (HTTPS)`

## Advantages of SEO:

- **1. More Website Traffic**
  - Brings more visitors from search engines.

- **2. Better Search Ranking**
  - Helps websites appear on the first page of search results.

- **3. Cost Effective**
  - Provides organic (free) traffic.

- **4. Builds Trust**
  - Higher-ranked websites are often trusted more by users.

- **5. Long-Term Results**
  - Good SEO can provide continuous traffic over time.

## Example:

`User searches → "Best laptop under 50000" → SEO helps a website appear in Google results.`

## Simple Line:

> **SEO is a technique used to improve a website's ranking and visibility on search engines.**
